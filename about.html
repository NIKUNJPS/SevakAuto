Our story

Built on repeat customers, not walk-in luck

We started in 2002 the way most honest workshops in Nashik start: one bay, a handful of tools, and a reputation carried by word of mouth from Saraswati Nagar to Nashik Road. Cars came in with the same complaints our roads produce every year — tired suspension after the monsoon, brakes worn thin by ghat driving, AC that gave up in April.

Over two decades the bays grew, the scanner arrived, and the job list widened to cover everything from denting and painting to pre-purchase inspections. What didn't change was the working rule: inspect the car properly, tell the owner exactly what it needs and what it will cost, and don't do a rupee of work beyond that without asking.

Today the workshop handles hatchbacks, sedans and SUVs across brands — because in the owner's words, each type of car has a solution here.

How we work

Four rules we don't bend

They sound simple. Sticking to them on a busy Saturday is what actually keeps customers coming back after twenty years.

01

Diagnose before quoting

Every car gets inspected, and where it matters, road-tested — so the estimate describes the real fault, not the obvious one.

02

Write the price down

Parts and labour, listed separately, before work starts. If something new turns up mid-job, you get a call, not a surprise.

03

Fit parts we'd fit on our own car

Genuine or OE-grade, your choice, clearly named on the estimate. Replaced parts are handed back on request.

04

Hand the car back clean

Seats covered, floor mats back in place, no greasy steering wheel. A tidy shop should return a tidy car.
