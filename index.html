What we do

One workshop, the whole job list

From a knocking front strut after the monsoon potholes to a full periodic service before a Trimbak or Saputara run — it gets handled in-house, by the same team, on the same floor.

Handicap modification

Hand controls, pedal adaptations and seating changes so drivers with disabilities can drive their own car safely and comfortably.

Paint job / custom paints

Full resprays, colour changes, two-tone and matte finishes — prepped properly so the paint lasts, not just shines for a month.

Cashless insurance claims

Accident repairs settled directly with your insurer — we coordinate the surveyor and paperwork, you pay only what the policy doesn't cover.

Facelifts

Give an older car the newer model's look — bumpers, grilles, LED lamps, alloys and trim, fitted to sit right.

Detailing

Foam wash, interior deep clean, rubbing, polishing and paint protection — the car handed back looking cared for.

Denting & painting

Dent removal, panel work and colour-matched paint — from parking-lot scrapes to full panel replacement.

Why drivers keep coming back

The bill is written before the spanner comes out

Most garage complaints start the same way: the job grew, nobody said anything, and the final amount came as a shock. Two decades in, we do it the other way round — inspect, list every part and labour line, get your nod, then start.

Itemised, upfront quotes

Parts and labour listed separately. Nothing extra gets added without a call to you first.

Technicians with real years behind them

Since 2002 our hands have been on Maharashtra roads' favourite cars — they know the failure patterns before the scanner confirms them.

Genuine or OE-grade parts only

You're told which grade is going in and what it costs. Old parts are handed back if you want to see them.

Quick, honest turnaround

Most routine services go out the same evening. If a part has to come from the market, you'll know by when.
