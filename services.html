How a job runs

From gate to handover

Same four steps whether it's an oil change or a full respray — unchanged since 2002.

01

Inspect

You describe the problem, we check the car — and road-test it when the fault is a noise or a feel rather than a warning light.

02

Quote

An itemised estimate: every part, every labour line. Questions answered before you commit to anything.

03

Repair

Work starts only after your approval. If something else turns up, you get a call with the revised cost first.

04

Hand over

Final check, a quick explanation of what was done, and the old parts back if you'd like to see them.

Pricing depends on your car's make, model and the parts grade you choose — which is why we quote per car instead of publishing one-size-fits-all rates. Quotes are free.

Tell us what it's doing

A short description or a video of the noise is usually enough for us to point you in the right direction.
